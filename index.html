Floating supplies are collected automatically when any floatilla boat passes close. Crew pick their own jobs: helm, fishing, trimming sails, rowing, repairs, lookout, sleep. Everyone eats and drinks. Each level is a stretch of the valley with the water at one height; the ways on are marked by orange flares. The voyage is saved when you arrive at each level.
